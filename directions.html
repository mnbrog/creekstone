Directions

Creekstone Retrievers is on a country farm at 1963 Glenwood Road, Morris, Alabama 35116 — just minutes north of Birmingham.

Our address

1963 Glenwood Road
Morris, Alabama 35116
  1. From Birmingham, take I-65 North toward Morris.
  2. Take the Morris exit and follow the signs into town.
  3. Continue to Glenwood Road and follow it to number 1963.
  4. Turn in at the farm — we will be expecting you.

Please call before you come

We are a working farm, so give us a ring first and we will make sure someone is here to meet you and show you the dogs.
