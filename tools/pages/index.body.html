Welcome

A well socialized beginning in life

Creekstone Retrievers was founded in 1985 by Cindy Stubbs. We sit on a beautiful country farm with a large lake and plenty of room for our Goldens to run and play.

We are dedicated to raising beautiful Golden Retriever puppies and giving each new family member a well socialized beginning in life. All of our Golden Retrievers have a great temperament and make wonderful companions for all ages.
